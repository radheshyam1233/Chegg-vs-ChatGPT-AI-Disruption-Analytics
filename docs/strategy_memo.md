# Strategy memo: from answer delivery to learning outcomes

## Problem
Chegg faced substantial pressure as generative AI offered alternative educational assistance. Correlation in timing does not prove AI was the sole cause of business declines.

## Financial evidence
Working quarterly series: revenue $205.19M (Q4 2022) to $143.48M (Q4 2024), a 30.07% decline; subscriber counts 5.008M to 3.637M, a 27.38% decline. Reconcile source definitions against Chegg's investor filings before external publication.

## Learning analytics
ASSISTments SQL compares first-observed help behavior with subsequent operational mastery (three consecutive correct first attempts on a skill). No causal conclusion is justified. Actual group metrics are not included until original CSV is processed and audited.

## Market opportunity
The ₹2,000 crore figure is a **hypothetical annual digital-product revenue opportunity**: 10 million potential paying learners × ₹2,000 annual spending. It is **not** the independently measured TAM of all Indian online test preparation. The underlying adoption and pricing assumptions require primary research.

## Product hypothesis
Test progressive hints, personalized practice, mastery tracking, and Indian competitive-exam content. Pilot with randomized assignment, later correctness, mastery, retention, and willingness-to-pay as metrics.

## Decision
Proceed to low-cost research and a controlled pilot; do not make an investment decision from unvalidated assumptions.
